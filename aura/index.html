Private by design

Yours to see, and yours to delete.

Your profile and memories live on servers we control. To write each reply, the recent conversation — and any photo you share, or your voice on a call — goes to our AI partner, xAI. For your voice notes, the recording of your side goes to xAI again after the call, to be transcribed. xAI processes it only for those jobs. It doesn’t keep it, and it doesn’t train on it.

We record your side of each call, never Aura’s, and keep it on our own servers until you delete that conversation or your account. We never sell your data, and you can see everything Aura remembers and delete it, permanently, anytime.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap your avatar at the top of Home to open your Profile. Near the bottom, open Privacy & account, tap Delete Account, and confirm. It takes effect right away.

Can’t open the app? Email privacy@vigyaan.ai from your account’s email address, and we’ll complete it within 30 days. What gets deleted, and when

Managing or cancelling Pro

Your free first year of Pro needs no card, so there is nothing to cancel. If you buy Pro, it’s billed through the App Store or Google Play, and you manage it in your store account:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aura.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Aura.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for. We don’t charge cancellation fees.

Signing in

Aura signs you in with Apple or Google, so there’s no separate Aura password. Use the same button you signed up with.

Is my data private?

Your account, history and memories are stored on infrastructure we control. To write a reply, the recent conversation — and any photo, or your voice on a call — goes to our AI partner xAI, which doesn’t keep it and doesn’t train on it. Memories marked sensitive are left out of everything we send to xAI, calls included. What you say on a call, xAI hears live, and your voice notes are made from those words. We never sell your data or use it for ads. The full Privacy Policy

Who can use Aura?

Aura is for adults 18 and older. She is a wellness and self-reflection companion — not a medical device, not a substitute for professional care, and not a crisis service.

Something isn’t working

Email support@vigyaan.ai with your device, its OS version and what happened. A screenshot helps.

If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out now. In the US, call or text 988. In India, call Vandrevala Foundation on +91 9999 666 555 or KIRAN on 1800-599-0019, at any hour. Anywhere else, find a line at findahelpline.com, or call your local emergency number.
