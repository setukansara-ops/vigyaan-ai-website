Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Aura is a private mental wellness companion built by Vigyaan LLC. You are always in control of your data. This page explains exactly how to delete your Aura account, or just specific data, and what happens to your information when you do.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Delete your account Help & support

Delete your entire account

You can permanently delete your Aura account and all associated data in either of these ways:

  1. In the app (fastest — takes effect immediately). Open Aura and tap your avatar at the top of the Home screen to open your Profile. Near the bottom, tap Privacy & account to open that section if it is closed. Then tap Delete Account and confirm. Your account and data are removed from our active systems right away.
  2. By email. If you can no longer open the app, email privacy@vigyaan.ai from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — email, display name, date of birth, and preferences
  • All chat and voice conversation history, including transcripts
  • Your voice calls — the recording of your side of every call, the call’s transcripts (including the word-by-word transcript), the measurements of how you spoke, and your voice notes, including the read of how you came across. Your “Your voice” page is emptied too.
  • Everything Aura remembered about you — memories, reflections, and life events
  • Any photos you shared and any details derived from them
  • Your reminders, goals, chosen mentors, and app settings

This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups: deleted data is removed from our active databases immediately and aged out of encrypted backups within 30 days.
  • Legally required records: transaction and subscription records may be retained by our payment processors and by the app stores (Apple, Google) as required by tax and accounting law. These records do not include your conversations, reflections, or anything Aura remembered about you.
  • Anonymized usage counts (e.g., total message counts, never including content) may be retained for product improvement.

Delete specific data (without deleting your account)

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific things. At any time you can:

  1. Delete individual conversations. Open your History, choose a conversation, and delete it. It leaves your History right away and waits in Recently deleted for 30 days, where you can restore it. After 30 days it is deleted for good, and removed from our backups within 30 days after that. What Aura has learned about you from it stays; you can remove that in your Memory tab (below).
  2. Delete a voice call. A voice call is part of its conversation in your History, so you delete it by deleting that conversation. The call leaves your “Your voice” page right away. While the conversation can still be restored, nothing of the call is deleted. When the conversation is deleted for good, 30 days later, the recording of your side of the call, the call’s transcripts (including the word-by-word transcript), the measurements of how you spoke, and the voice notes, including the read of how you came across, are deleted with it. Voice notes already written for your other calls stay as they are, even where they compare those calls with the deleted one. We keep a basic record that the call happened — when it started, how long it lasted, and a few technical details, with no audio and no words — until you delete your account.
  3. Delete individual memories or life events. In your Memory tab, remove any item. Deleted memories sit in a 30-day trash so you can restore one removed by mistake, then are purged permanently.
  4. Prefer email? Write to privacy@vigyaan.ai describing what you'd like removed, and we'll take care of it within 30 days.

Questions

For anything about your data or these steps, email privacy@vigyaan.ai. You can also review our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
