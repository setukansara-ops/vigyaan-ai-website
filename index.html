The flagship
Auraby Vigyaan

A companion, not a feed.

Talk by text or voice. Aura quietly remembers the things that matter — the people you mention, what you're working toward, the moments worth keeping — and brings them back like a friend would: a callback, a morning check-in, an occasional short letter. No feed, no follower counts, no ads. Just a warm presence that knows your story because you told it.

✦

She remembers

Memory that carries across every conversation — visible to you, editable by you, deletable by you.

  • Morning check-ins & evening wind-downs
  • Guided breathing when you need a minute
  • Your sky: memories light a constellation
🔒

She respects

Your data stays on infrastructure we control, and is never sold, never shown to advertisers, never used to train anyone's AI.

  • Sensitive memories never leave our servers
  • Photos are read on our own hardware, not a third-party AI
  • Zero-data-retention terms with our AI provider
🌙

She restrains

Aura is built against dependence — the opposite of engagement-at-all-costs.

  • Her own nudges: at most one a day, four a week
  • Backs off when you don't respond
  • No push notifications — she never pings you
Honest lane, always.

Aura is a wellness companion for adults 18 and over. She is not therapy, not diagnosis, not treatment, and not crisis care — and she says so plainly, in the app. If a conversation shows signs of crisis, Aura's response includes professional 24/7 helplines (988 in the US, and international lines elsewhere). If you're in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

The studio

Small on purpose.

Vigyaan is a founder-operated studio. One person designed, built, and shipped Aura end to end — Flutter on both app stores, a FastAPI backend, and a privacy-first AI architecture where the sensitive parts never leave hardware we control. That shipped product is our credentials; we don't have a wall of logos, and we won't invent one.

Privacy

What we don't do is the feature.

No ads, ever

No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no ad networks anywhere near your words.

No data selling

We don't sell, rent, or trade personal data. There is no data-broker anywhere in this story.

No imports

No contact scraping, no social-media hookups. Aura knows only what you choose to tell her.

Your exit, guaranteed

Delete your account from inside the app — conversations, memories, profile. Encrypted backups roll off within 14 days.
