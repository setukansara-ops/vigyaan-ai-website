How ordering works

Request now. We confirm before any payment.

  1. Add to your request list

    Pick single oils or a trio. Nothing is charged, and your list stays in this browser.

  2. Send it by email

    Your list opens as an email to setu@vigyaan.ai from your own mail app, with your name and email.

  3. We write back

    We confirm stock, New Jersey pickup or shipping, and only then payment.

This is a request list, not a live warehouse. We never charge a card on this site.
