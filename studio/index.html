The studio

Small on purpose.

Vigyaan is a founder-operated house in New Jersey. One person shipped Aura end to end. The attar line is the next product we will put in someone's hand — not a slide deck.

What we make

Software that treats memory as care. Oils that treat scent as a daily object, not a cloud of alcohol. If a third line earns a place, it will meet the same bar: honest label, small batch, ours.

How the oils are made

Juice from more than one workshop in India. Labels, cartons, and US compliance from here. We do not sit on a single factory, and we do not print “Made in USA” on a filled import.

What we will not do

Fake hour-counts. Incomplete ingredient lines. Selling a bottle before we have smelled the batch. Charging a card on this site before we can ship.
